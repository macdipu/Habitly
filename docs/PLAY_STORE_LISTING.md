## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Habitly is a fully offline, privacy-first habit tracker. No sign-up, no
cloud, no ads — everything stays on your phone.

BUILD ROUTINES THAT FIT YOUR LIFE
Daily habits, specific weekdays, weekly quotas, or count-based goals — set
the shape that actually matches how you live, instead of forcing every
habit into the same daily checkbox.

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOUR TIME
Local notifications only, nothing ever leaves your device. Quiet hours
suppress reminders during the times you set, instead of nagging you when
you've said not to.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS AT A GLANCE
A full calendar view lets you drill into any day, and streak/adherence
tracking shows your progress without turning a missed day into a scored
failure.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no backend server, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK. Export or back
up your data to a file whenever you want — it never touches a server
Habitly operates.

NO PRESSURE, NO SHAME
Missed days read as neutral, not red or "broken." Skips don't wipe out a
streak the way they do in most habit apps.

LIGHT & DARK
Follows your system theme with a warm, calm palette designed to be easy to
look at every day.

Habitly is open source. Read the code, file an issue, or contribute:
https://github.com/macdipu/Habitly
```
