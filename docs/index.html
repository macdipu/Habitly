Build habits, not accounts.

Habitly is a fully offline, privacy-first habit tracker. No sign-up, no cloud, no ads — everything stays on your phone.

Download for Android View source

Free, open source, APK on GitHub Releases. Not yet on Google Play.

100% offline No account required No ads or tracking Open source

Flexible schedules

Daily, specific weekdays, weekly quotas, or count-based habits — set the shape that actually fits your routine.

Reminders that respect quiet hours

Local notifications only — nothing ever leaves your device. Quiet hours suppress reminders instead of nagging you back.

Calendar & insights

See every day at a glance, drill into any date, and track streaks and adherence without judgment for a skipped day.

Your data stays yours

No account, no backend, no analytics. Export or back up your data as a local file whenever you want.

No pressure, no shame

Missed days read as neutral, not red or scored as failure. Skips don't break streaks the way they should.

Light & dark

Follows your system theme with a warm, calm palette designed to be easy to look at every day.
